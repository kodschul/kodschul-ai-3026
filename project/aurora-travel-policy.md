# Aurora Logistics: Travel and Expense Policy

- Fictional company and fictional policy, used only for this course
- Version: 2026-1 (training edition)
- Scope: all employees on business travel
- All amounts in EUR, per person

## Section 1: Flights and accommodation

- Flights under 3 hours: economy class only
- Flights of 3 hours or more: premium economy allowed
- Business class: only with prior manager approval, and only for flights of 6 hours or more
- Hotel limit per night, by city tier:
  - Tier 1 cities (Munich, Frankfurt, Hamburg, Vienna, Zurich): EUR 180
  - Tier 2 (all other cities): EUR 120
- Hotel breakfast is reimbursed only when included in the room rate
- Not covered by this policy: minibar, in-room entertainment, spa and gym fees (treated as personal costs)

## Section 2: Ground transport

- Taxi and ride-hailing: up to EUR 60 per ride
- A receipt is required for every ride
- Public transport: reimbursed in full with a ticket
- Rental cars: only with prior manager approval
- Private car: EUR 0.42 per kilometer, with a trip log

## Section 3: Meals (per diem)

- Per diem for a full travel day: EUR 40
- Reduced to EUR 20 for the arrival and departure day
- No per diem for meals already paid by the client or the organizer
- Alcohol is never reimbursed

## Section 4: Approvals and thresholds

- Claim total up to EUR 500: no extra approval step
- Claim total above EUR 500 and up to EUR 2,000: line manager approval required
- Claim total above EUR 2,000: line manager and finance approval required
- Every payout is released by a person, never by an automated check
- Job title or seniority does not change these thresholds

## Section 5: Submission

- Submit within 30 days after the trip ends
- Attach one receipt per item above EUR 25
- Foreign-currency receipts are converted at the rate of the receipt date
- Late claims need a written reason and manager approval
