# Lab 6.2: Exercise: Write a Transfer Note

**Changes the TravelDesk baseline?** No. Transfer exercise on a use case from the own work.

## Starting point

- One agent idea from the own work; no company or customer data is written down
- File: `../../project/transfer-note.md`

## Tasks

1. Name a use case from the own work.
2. Pick the integration option closest to its main constraint (channel, knowledge,
   orchestration, application, or cross-team agents).
3. Write the first step.
4. Name one risk to resolve first.

## Checkpoint

- All five fields of the template are filled
- The chosen option is justified by a constraint, not by preference

## Completion criteria

- A transfer note of at most half a page exists
- The first step can be started within one week
- The risk is concrete: a quota, an access question, or a document owner

## Extension

- Exchange notes with a partner and name one assumption the partner's note makes without
  evidence

## Fallback

- Without an own use case, the Aurora TravelDesk case is used and the note describes its
  rollout to Teams
